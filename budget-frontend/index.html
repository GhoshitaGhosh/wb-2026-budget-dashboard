Data notes

What the numbers mean

Transparency is part of the interface: uncertainty and incompleteness are shown rather than hidden.

Allocation ≠ spending

A budget estimate authorises a provision. It does not show how much was released or actually spent.

Units are canonical

Source department values are stored as integer thousand rupees, then converted to crore in one shared formatter.

Relationships may span rows

Exact, grouped, alias, and reviewed aggregate matches are distinct. Every included budget code remains visible.

Missing does not mean zero

Not stated, unmatched, zero, and token provisions are represented as different data states.
